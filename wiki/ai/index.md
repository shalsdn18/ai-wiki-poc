## LLM Code Generation in Hobby Communities: Conflict Analysis & Specification-Driven Solution

Hobby programming communities prioritize the process of mastering domain knowledge over the final product. LLM-generated code, often based on shallow understanding, bypasses this process, leading to an influx of low-quality contributions. This creates an asymmetric code review bottleneck and burnout for open-source maintainers, as the core bottleneck in development is often architectural consensus, not coding speed. To address this, a Specification-Driven Generation workflow is proposed, enforcing markdown-based specifications and constraints. This shifts the cost from post-code verification to upfront architectural design and specification writing, aiming to eliminate "AI Smell" and ensure maintainability.
